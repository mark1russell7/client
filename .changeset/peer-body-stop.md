The peer accepts a bare JSON value and obeys `bodyLimit` (roadmap R5). Before, its strict JSON parser refused a number, a string or `null` with 400, and its limit of 100 kB replaced `bodyLimit`. The HTTP transport config of the peer takes `bodyLimit`. `peer.stop()` closes the HTTP server that the peer made for its WebSocket transport, so the port is free again. `mark --server` and the `server` bin stop the peer before they exit, with a limit of 10 seconds, and a second signal exits at once.
