Typed durations (roadmap R11). This change breaks the API. Each public option that is a time takes a `Duration` of `@mark1russell7/quantity`, made with `ms()`, `seconds()`, `minutes()` or `hours()` of `@procedures/client`. A plain number is a type error, and at run time the option throws a `TypeError` that names it. This covers the timeouts and `resilience()`, the retry delays, the cache, the circuit breaker, the rate limit, the HTTP, WebSocket and mock transports, the WebSocket server heartbeat, `timeoutInterceptor({ limit })` (was `ms`), `hostPolicy({ timeout })` (was `timeoutMs`), the idempotency cache (`ttl`, was `ttlMs`), `ApiStorage`, `HybridStorage`, the synced registry and `connect()` of the peer. The per-call overrides in a context (`timeout`, `retry.delay`, `cache.ttl`) and the `route()` overrides (`timeout.overall`, was `timeout.ms`) take durations too. The wire and the inputs of procedures keep milliseconds.

Migration: `createOverallTimeoutMiddleware({ overall: 5000 })` becomes `createOverallTimeoutMiddleware({ overall: seconds(5) })`.
