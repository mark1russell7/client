Cancellation scopes and a graceful stop (roadmap R14). `ProcedureServer.stop({ grace })` refuses new calls with a retryable `UNAVAILABLE` and lets the calls in flight end until the grace time ends, then aborts them (`peer.stop({ grace })` passes it; `mark --server` and the `server` bin give 5 seconds). The signal of each call joins its request and the server, and `callsInFlight` counts the calls, streams included. `exec()` and `execStream()` take a `signal`. `LocalTransport` takes `interceptors`, `capability` and `clearanceOrder`, and gives a call the deadline that its client sends, so a client timeout ends a local handler too. `mark` aborts a command in its own process on the first Ctrl+C. A remote leaf of `route()` and `server.call` send the deadline of their caller, and `server.call` relays the signal.
